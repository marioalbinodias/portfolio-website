about

Born and raised in Goa, India, I started my professional journey with a degree in Computer Engineering before discovering my passion for design. Over the years, I've worked across graphic design, motion graphics and UI/UX, designing digital experiences for websites, mobile applications and a variety of visual mediums.

My work has always involved working closely with technology teams, which naturally led me to explore the development side of digital experiences. Today, I combine my design background with frontend development to design and build responsive, interactive websites and interfaces.

I've developed much of my frontend knowledge through hands-on projects, practical experience and self-directed learning using online resources, documentation and tutorials. I enjoy understanding how things work beyond the visual layer and turning ideas and designs into functional experiences using HTML, SCSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript and animation tools such as GSAP.

I'm now looking to bring this combination of design, frontend development and motion into a role where I can contribute to meaningful digital products while continuing to grow as a designer and developer.
